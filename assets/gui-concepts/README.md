# GUI Concepts

These images explore **future** Ainix interaction models across desktop, touch,
mobile, AR, and VR contexts.

> **These are product-vision concept art, not screenshots of current software.**
> The working prototype today is a comparatively simple hosted canvas shell —
> see `assets/development-updates/` for what actually runs. The polished
> interfaces below depict where the project aims to go, not what exists.

## Files

- `desktop-infinite-canvas.png` — concept of the infinite semantic canvas on desktop
- `touchscreen-canvas.png` — concept of a touch-first canvas surface
- `mobile-companion.png` — concept of a mobile companion view
- `ar-context-overlay.png` — concept of an AR context overlay
- `vr-spatial-workspace.png` — concept of a VR spatial workspace
- `cross-media-suite.png` — concept of the experience across screens and devices

Recommended wording: "Ainix future-interface concept across everyday screens,
touch surfaces, and spatial devices — not current software."
